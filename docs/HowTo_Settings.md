    "```\n",
    "cd unieat\n",
    "```\n",

    "```\n",
    "python manage.py runserver\n",

    "验证完成后，关闭服务器（按CTRL+C），安装mysql的python驱动：\n",

    "```\n",
    "接下来创建并连接数据库\n",
    "- 打开MySql的workbench\n",
